# Prompt Logic

## Objective

The objective of this prompt is to generate professional, engaging, and website-ready copy for a local cloud kitchen using AI. The content is designed to highlight the business's unique value, build customer trust, and encourage online orders.


## Business Details

- **Business Name:** Divine Cuisine
- **Website Name:** Divine Cuisine
- **Tagline:** Homely Food, Divine Bite
- **Business Type:** Cloud Kitchen
- **Location:** Mangalore, Karnataka


## Prompt Structure

The prompt was designed with the following sections:

1. Business Name
2. Website Name
3. Tagline
4. Business Type
5. Location
6. Business Description
7. Target Audience
8. Desired Tone
9. Required Website Sections
10. Call-to-Action Requirements

This structured approach helps the AI understand the business clearly and generate accurate, relevant, and consistent website content.


## Prompt Engineering Approach

Instead of asking the AI to "write website content," a detailed and structured prompt was used.

The prompt included:
- Clear business information
- Target audience
- Business goals
- Preferred writing style
- Required website sections

This approach produces more relevant and professional results.


## AI Tool Used

- ChatGPT (GPT-5.5)


## Output Generated

The AI successfully generated:

- Homepage Copy
- Services Page Content
- Call-to-Action (CTA) Sections

The content is simple, persuasive, and ready to be used on a real business website.



## Outcome

Using a structured prompt improved the quality of the generated website copy. The output reflects the identity of Divine Cuisine and creates a warm, trustworthy impression for potential customers.
